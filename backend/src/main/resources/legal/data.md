# Telefam Data Policy

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

This Data Policy provides additional practical information about how Telefam handles account information, content, communications, technical information, retention, and user requests. It should be read together with the Telefam Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## 1. Categories of Data

Telefam may process account information such as email address, phone number, username, name, profile information, and account preferences.

Telefam may also process content and activity such as posts, photos, videos, comments, messages, reactions, follows, saves, shares, searches, views, reports, and other interactions required to provide the Service.

Technical and security information may include device and operating-system information, app version, IP address, session information, diagnostic information, and other information reasonably necessary to maintain the Service and protect accounts.

Optional features may involve additional information, including location, contacts, transactions, monetization, marketplace, verification, or support information.

## 2. Content Storage and Delivery

User content may be stored and processed using infrastructure and service providers needed to operate Telefam. Media may be processed into formats suitable for reliable delivery and may be subject to automated safety, quality, or operational processing.

Content visibility is determined by the applicable feature and user settings. Public content may be accessible to other users, while restricted content is handled according to the relevant privacy controls and access rules.

## 3. Communications

Communication features may use encryption and other safeguards appropriate to the feature. Certain operational information may be processed to deliver communications, maintain service reliability, support account security, prevent abuse, and comply with law.

## 4. Data Sharing

Telefam does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers that help operate the platform, with other users when you intentionally make information available to them, or when disclosure is required or permitted for legitimate legal, safety, security, or operational reasons.

## 5. Retention and Deletion

Information is generally retained while an account or feature remains active and for additional periods where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, backups, or other legitimate purposes.

Account deletion does not necessarily result in immediate deletion from every backup or legally retained record.

## 6. User Requests

Depending on applicable law, users may request access, correction, export, restriction, or deletion of eligible personal information. Requests can be made through available account controls or by contacting **privacy@telefam.app**.

## 7. Security Information

Telefam uses technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. This document does not publish internal security architecture, authentication implementation details, access-control configuration, abuse-detection logic, or other operational security information.
