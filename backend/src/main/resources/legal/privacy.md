# Telefam Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Telefam ("Telefam," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use our services.

We aim to collect information that is reasonably necessary to provide, secure, personalize, and improve Telefam. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers.

## 1. Information We Collect

### 1.1 Account and profile information

Depending on the features you use, we may collect:

- Email address.
- Phone number.
- Full or display name.
- Username and profile identifier.
- Profile photo and other profile information.
- Date of birth or age information where required for eligibility, safety, or legal compliance.
- Country, city, or other location information that you choose to provide.
- Bio, website, and other profile details.
- Account preferences and settings.
- Verification or support information when you request certain services or account assistance.

### 1.2 Content you create or share

We collect and process content you choose to provide through Telefam, including:

- Posts, captions, photos, videos, and other media.
- Comments and replies.
- Likes, reactions, saves, shares, and reshares.
- Stories, where available.
- Profile information and other content you publish.
- Messages and communications sent through Telefam.
- Reports, appeals, feedback, and support requests.
- Content associated with creator, marketplace, monetization, or other optional features when you use them.

The visibility of content depends on the feature and the settings you select.

### 1.3 Social and interaction information

We may collect information about how you interact with other users and features, including follows, connections, requests, blocks, comments, reactions, shares, searches, views, and other interactions necessary to operate social features and personalize the Service.

### 1.4 Device and technical information

When you use Telefam, we may receive technical information such as:

- Device type and model.
- Operating system and app version.
- Language and general regional settings.
- Network and connection information.
- IP address and related security information.
- Session information and authentication-related events.
- Crash, performance, and diagnostic information.
- Information reasonably necessary to detect abuse, protect accounts, and maintain service reliability.

### 1.5 Location information

If you choose to provide or permit location information for a feature, we may process the relevant location information to provide that feature. You can control applicable location permissions through your device or Telefam settings where those controls are available.

We do not require precise location information merely to use ordinary social features unless a particular feature requires it and you choose to use that feature.

### 1.6 Contacts and discovery information

If you enable contact discovery, Telefam may process contact information from your device for the purpose of helping you find people you already know who use Telefam, subject to the permissions and controls provided to you.

You can disable contact access through your device or available Telefam settings. Contact discovery may involve privacy-preserving processing designed to reduce unnecessary exposure of contact information.

### 1.7 Transaction and monetization information

If you use purchases, subscriptions, gifts, creator monetization, marketplace, or payout features, we may process information necessary to complete and secure those activities, such as transaction records, payment status, payout information, verification information, and information provided by payment or financial-service providers.

Payment credentials may be handled directly by applicable payment providers rather than stored by Telefam where appropriate.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information for purposes including:

- Creating and managing accounts.
- Authenticating users and maintaining account security.
- Providing profiles, feeds, messaging, posting, search, discovery, and other requested features.
- Delivering and displaying content according to user settings.
- Processing uploads and preparing media for reliable delivery.
- Personalizing feeds, recommendations, and feature experiences.
- Measuring service performance and improving reliability.
- Detecting spam, fraud, abuse, malicious activity, and violations of our rules.
- Protecting users, the Service, and our infrastructure.
- Responding to reports, appeals, and support requests.
- Processing purchases, subscriptions, monetization, and payouts where available.
- Communicating with you about your account, security, service updates, and important changes.
- Complying with legal obligations and responding to lawful requests.
- Establishing, exercising, or defending legal claims.

## 3. How We Use Content and Interactions

Telefam processes posts, interactions, and other activity to provide social functionality and to help maintain a safe and useful platform. For example, information about interactions may be used to display relevant content, operate notifications, maintain counts and feeds, enforce user settings, and detect abuse.

We do not use the existence of a feature or security control as a promise that every abuse attempt will be prevented.

## 4. Messages and Communications

Depending on the communication feature, messages may be protected using encryption and access controls designed to limit unauthorized access. Certain service information may still be processed to deliver messages, maintain accounts, provide requested features, prevent abuse, or comply with law.

You should not assume that any communication is completely private from its recipients. Recipients may copy, screenshot, record, forward, or otherwise preserve information you send them.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information.

We may share information in the following circumstances:

### Service providers

We may use trusted providers for hosting, storage, databases, email delivery, authentication, communications, media processing, analytics, security, customer support, payment processing, and other services needed to operate Telefam. Providers receive information reasonably necessary to perform their services and are expected to handle it under applicable contractual or legal requirements.

### Other users and the public

Information you intentionally publish publicly may be visible to other users or the public according to the feature and your settings.

### Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information where reasonably necessary to comply with law, valid legal process, enforce our Terms, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, protect users or the public, or protect our rights and property.

### Business transfers

If Telefam or substantially all of its assets are involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, reorganization, sale, or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law and appropriate protections.

## 6. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational, and administrative measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for protecting your credentials, using a secure device, and notifying us through appropriate support channels if you believe your account has been compromised.

For security reasons, this Privacy Policy intentionally does not describe internal security architecture, defensive controls, detection thresholds, private endpoints, or other information that could increase risk to users or the Service if publicly disclosed.

## 7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, including providing the Service, maintaining security, resolving disputes, enforcing our rules, and complying with legal obligations.

When you delete your account or content, we may remove it from active systems within a reasonable period. Some information may remain for longer where necessary for backups, fraud prevention, security, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or other lawful purposes.

Retention periods may differ depending on the type of information and the reason it is retained.

## 8. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to:

- Access information we hold about you.
- Correct inaccurate information.
- Request deletion of personal information.
- Request a copy or export of certain information.
- Object to or restrict certain processing.
- Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
- Control certain privacy, messaging, interaction, and discovery settings.

Some rights are subject to legal exceptions. You can use available account and privacy controls in Telefam or contact us for assistance.

## 9. Children's Privacy

Telefam is not intended for users below the minimum age permitted by applicable law. Where applicable law requires parental or guardian authorization for a minor to use a service or for certain data processing, we will apply the requirements that govern that situation.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of applicable requirements, contact us so that we can review the situation.

## 10. International Processing

Telefam and its service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for international transfers and processing.

## 11. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Telefam may use necessary local storage, identifiers, cookies, or similar technologies where needed to maintain sessions, remember preferences, provide requested functionality, measure performance, or protect the Service. Where applicable, additional choices will be provided through device, browser, or service settings.

## 12. Third-Party Services

Telefam may rely on third-party services to provide specific functionality. Those providers may have their own privacy policies and terms. We encourage you to review the applicable information when you use third-party features.
