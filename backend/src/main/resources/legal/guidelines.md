# Telefam Community Guidelines

**Last updated: 1 October 2026**

Telefam is intended to be a place where people can communicate, share content, discover communities, and participate safely. These Community Guidelines explain the main types of behavior and content that are not allowed.

## 1. Safety and Respect

Do not use Telefam to:

- Threaten or encourage serious physical harm.
- Harass, stalk, intimidate, or repeatedly target another person.
- Promote hatred or dehumanizing attacks against protected groups.
- Encourage dangerous or unlawful activity.
- Sexualize or exploit minors.
- Facilitate trafficking, exploitation, or abuse.

## 2. Privacy

Do not publish another person's private or sensitive information without appropriate authorization. Do not share private communications, intimate material, account credentials, or other personal information in a way that violates another person's privacy or safety.

## 3. Authenticity and Deception

Do not impersonate others in a deceptive way, create fraudulent accounts, manipulate identity information, or coordinate deceptive activity intended to mislead users.

Parody, fan, commentary, or similar accounts should not falsely imply official affiliation.

## 4. Spam, Scams, and Manipulation

Do not use Telefam for:

- Phishing or credential theft.
- Fraudulent offers or transactions.
- Pyramid or deceptive schemes.
- Mass unsolicited messages.
- Fake engagement or coordinated manipulation of followers, likes, views, comments, or other platform signals.
- Repeated abusive automation intended to evade normal platform limits.

## 5. Illegal or Harmful Content

Content that facilitates serious unlawful conduct, exploitation, abuse, or dangerous activity may be removed or restricted. Where legally required or appropriate, relevant information may be referred to competent authorities.

## 6. Intellectual Property

Do not upload or distribute material that infringes another person's copyright, trademark, or other intellectual-property rights. You should have the rights or permissions needed for content you publish.

## 7. Enforcement

We may remove content, limit features, restrict distribution, suspend accounts, or terminate accounts when we determine that action is reasonably necessary under our rules, applicable law, or user-safety requirements.

We may consider severity, context, repetition, account history, and potential harm. Enforcement systems and review processes may change as the Service develops.

## 8. Appeals

Where an appeal process is available, users may submit an appeal through the relevant in-app support or appeal flow. We may request information reasonably necessary to review an appeal.

## 9. Reporting

Use the available reporting tools to report content or behavior that violates these Guidelines. For immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency or law-enforcement service in your location.
